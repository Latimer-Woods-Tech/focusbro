## Licences in one paragraph

The field recordings come from **radio aporee ::: maps**, where each recordist chooses the licence at upload and may choose "PUBLIC DOMAIN" (aporee.org/maps/info: *"select a licence under which the recording should be published, or choose PUBLIC DOMAIN"*). Every recording used here is one its recordist dedicated to the public domain; the Internet Archive mirror records that choice as Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0 in the item's `licenseurl`. No attribution is required — it is given anyway. Keys, Fan and the Bowl strikes were generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects on a paid plan (commercial use permitted); the takes are archived in R2 under `sources/elevenlabs/` because a generation cannot be repeated byte-for-byte. The Drone is our own synthesis (CC0). The three "hush" noises are synthesised in the browser and ship no file.
