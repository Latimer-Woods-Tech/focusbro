I have ADHD. I set reminders, then swiped them away without reading them. So I built the thing I needed: a friend who asks, at the moment I said, "did it happen?"

That's FocusBro. Tell me one thing and a time. I'll check in right then. You were never in trouble here.

GIVE YOUR WORD
• Type what you're putting off and pick a time: "in 30 min", "tomorrow 9am", "Saturday".
• At that moment, a check-in lands on your phone. It's scheduled on the phone itself, so it still arrives when you're offline.
• Answer in one tap: I did it · I'm on it · Not yet · Move it · Set it down.
• Not yet? No problem. Pick a new time and I'll be back.
• Make it a rhythm: every day or weekdays, same time, same warm nudge.
• Choose my tone: calm ally or hype.
• I count the words you keep, and only those.

SEE YOUR WEEK
• Words kept in a row, the days you showed up, and your momentum over the last two weeks.
• Add a note when you keep a word ("felt lighter after") and read it back later.
• A weekly report you can copy or share with a coach if you have one.

FOCUS TIMER
• 25-minute focus blocks with short breaks.
• Your sounds fade out at the break bell and come back when the next block starts.
• Focus sprints: a shared sprint starts at the top of every hour, and the timer shows how many people are focusing with you right now.
• Keep your screen awake while you work. Your sounds keep playing with the screen off.

SOUNDS
• Recorded rain, ocean, forest, stream, night, wind, café, fireplace, keyboard, fan, train, singing bowl and more.
• Layer as many as you like and set the volume.
• Or start from a ready blend: Deep work, Block it out, Wind down, Not alone, Rainy café, Sleep.

WHEN YOU NEED A RESET
• Breathing pacer: box, 4-7-8 and tactical breathing.
• 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a place to park stray thoughts, a short task list, quick notes and a 20-20-20 eye-break reminder.
